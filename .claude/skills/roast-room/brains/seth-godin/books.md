# Books: Seth Godin

His own books as the corpus describes them, from the extraction notes on all 89 sources. He says he packaged about 120 books before Permission Marketing, his first "real" solo book (S075), and he says 21 of his books in a row were bestsellers (S023). Book text was not fetched; summaries are in my own words, from what he and his hosts say about them. Most-discussed first.

## This Is Strategy: Make Better Plans (2024)
- **Core thesis:** Most people have tactics, goals, and an inbox, not a strategy. Strategy is a philosophy of becoming: a stated point of view about who you serve and the change you seek, built to survive a changing world. A compass, not a map.
- **Key frameworks:** the four threads (systems, time, games, empathy); who's it for and what's it for; pick your customers, pick your future, plus the other choices (competition, validation, distribution); kindling and logs; insatiable desire versus a momentary gap; network effect test; circle of now and circle of us; good decision versus good outcome; strategy that is simple to describe and hard to stick to. Written as about 297 short riffs with no page numbers, plus a list of more than 500 strategy questions and a strategy card deck.
- **Taught in sources:** S007, S008, S012, S018, S019, S021, S023, S024, S027, S030, S036, S037, S042, S053, S061, S062, S064, S075.

## Purple Cow (2003)
- **Core thesis:** Average products for average people stopped working; make something remarkable, meaning worth making a remark about, and the people who care will spread it. The person who touches it decides if it is remarkable, not the maker.
- **Key frameworks:** remarkable versus very good; build the reason to talk into the product; talking about you serves the teller's status. He self-published it first, shipped the first 10,000 copies in a milk carton, and says it sold over a million because it was distinctive for some people and not others; he says he wrote it for 5,000 readers.
- **Taught in sources:** S011, S012, S013, S016, S019, S027, S028, S031, S036, S037, S046, S049, S054, S057, S075.

## The Dip (2007)
- **Core thesis:** Everything worth doing has a hard part between starting and getting good, and that hard part is what keeps the other side scarce. Winners quit the right things: before they start or after the dip, never in the middle. He calls it his shortest book with the most impact per page; publishers said 96 pages was too short.
- **Key frameworks:** dip versus cul-de-sac or slope; check you can cross before you start; sunk costs as a gift from your former self; set the quit point in advance; avoid being a wandering generality.
- **Taught in sources:** S009, S011, S013, S014, S017, S018, S021, S022, S025, S028, S035, S038, S049, S057, S075.

## Permission Marketing (1999)
- **Core thesis:** Interruption advertising is losing. Earn the privilege of sending anticipated, personal, and relevant messages to people who want them, and that permission becomes the company's core asset. He says it helped create email marketing as an industry, and he chose not to write a sequel.
- **Key frameworks:** dating your prospects instead of interrupting strangers; turn strangers into friends and friends into customers; own the list, protect it, do things for people, not to them; permission as the new monopoly.
- **Taught in sources:** S004, S005, S007, S012, S016, S019, S020, S025, S027, S031, S075, S078, S083, S086.

## This Is Marketing (2018)
- **Core thesis:** Marketing is making change happen for a specific group: telling a true story to the smallest viable audience, in a way that helps them get where they want to go, so they tell others. Built from a workshop with more than 6,000 students; he calls it more tactical than his usual books and says in 2026 it is more relevant now.
- **Key frameworks:** smallest viable audience; the five steps of marketing; tension; status, affiliation, and dominance; people like us do things like this; psychographics over demographics; the "for people who believe" positioning sentence; the positioning grid.
- **Taught in sources:** S003, S009, S011, S017, S033, S034, S039, S040, S054, S075.

## Linchpin (2010)
- **Core thesis:** The industrial deal (follow instructions, get paid) is gone. The valuable person is the one who would be hard to live without, who does human, unpredictable, emotionally risky work that no manual covers.
- **Key frameworks:** cog versus linchpin; the seven ways linchpins add value; art as a gift; the lizard brain and the resistance; if your job can be written down, someone cheaper will do it.
- **Taught in sources:** S013, S014, S021, S022, S026, S027, S035, S050, S075.

## The Knot (2026)
- **Core thesis:** People get stuck because they want two things that cannot coexist, and hidden entanglements (old feelings, fears, obligations) keep the knot tight. Separate problems you can solve from situations you must accept, find the but, and tell the truth. Out in September 2026.
- **Key frameworks:** problem versus situation; buts into ands; the post at the center of the Gordian knot; mission as what guides your feedback loop; the three questions (how can I help, what is stopping me, what am I unwilling to give up).
- **Taught in sources:** S009, S011, S013, S019, S025, S043, S047, S051.

## The Song of Significance (2023)
- **Core thesis:** A manifesto for teams after the industrial age. People want significance, respect, and work they are proud of, not perks; leaders create the conditions for it instead of managing people like machines. Built on a survey of about 10,000 people about their best job.
- **Key frameworks:** management versus leadership; page 19 thinking; criticize the work, not the worker; false proxies in hiring; trust and stakes; let's get real or let's not play.
- **Taught in sources:** S005, S028, S032, S045, S046, S050, S052.

## The Practice: Shipping Creative Work (2020)
- **Core thesis:** Creative work comes from a daily practice of shipping generous work, not from talent or waiting for the muse. Skills, including attitudes, are choices that can be learned. Its working title was "Trust Yourself".
- **Key frameworks:** merely ship it, meeting spec without attachment; throwing, not catching; bad ideas before good taste; hack work owned as hack work.
- **Taught in sources:** S004, S020, S029, S041, S075.

## Tribes (2008)
- **Core thesis:** People want to belong to groups connected by an idea, a culture, and a leader, and anyone can lead one. He later changed this view (see principles.md): by 2026 he says you do not have a tribe; at best you narrate for one that would exist without you.
- **Key frameworks:** a tribe is a group connected by a culture, an idea, a costume, a leader, and a goal; connect people who want to connect; the Beatles did not invent teenagers.
- **Taught in sources:** S002, S016, S019, S038, S047, S075.

## All Marketers Are Liars (2005)
- **Core thesis:** People buy the story they tell themselves, so marketing works by matching a true story to an existing worldview. He calls the title lousy for a really important idea.
- **Key frameworks:** worldview first; start with what people already believe; story over facts.
- **Taught in sources:** S017, S031, S075.

## Survival Is Not Enough (2002)
- **Core thesis:** Organizations should treat change and evolution as an asset. He calls it an indulgent book that did not sell, which taught him to write for readers rather than for himself; he still calls it a favorite.
- **Key frameworks:** none used in the corpus beyond the lesson about writing for the reader.
- **Taught in sources:** S004, S012, S013, S019, S025, S035.

## What to Do When It's Your Turn (2015)
- **Core thesis:** A self-published push to stop waiting to be picked and go do the work. He says it sold over 150,000 copies without being in a store.
- **Key frameworks:** pick yourself; the Annie Kenney story.
- **Taught in sources:** S001, S035, S039, S048.

## The Carbon Almanac (2022)
- **Core thesis:** A volunteer-built, footnoted book of climate facts, with solutions after the problems. For this seat it matters as a lesson in enrollment: he says organizing it taught him that information alone does not change habits.
- **Key frameworks:** enrollment of volunteers; information does not change behavior, pricing does.
- **Taught in sources:** S005, S009, S010, S019, S028, S030, S046.

## Stop Stealing Dreams (2012)
- **Core thesis:** Public school is an industrial-age artifact built to produce compliant workers. Off-topic for this seat.
- **Taught in sources:** S002.

## Mentioned only by hosts
- **Poke the Box (2011)** and **The Icarus Deception (2012)** are named by hosts (S039, S023) with no argument from him.

## Other written work used in the corpus
- **seths.blog:** a daily post for more than 20 years; the business essays in the corpus run from 2002 to 2026 (S055 to S089).
- **The Strategy Questions** (S062) and the strategy card deck (S007, S027): companion tools to This Is Strategy.
- **The Builder's Creed** (S058): 32 promises to yourself, the work, each other, and the future.
- **altMBA** (S002, S004): a cohort program built on shipping public projects, not a book.
