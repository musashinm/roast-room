## Where his views shifted or pull against each other

- **Tribes: build one, or narrate for one that already exists.** In 2008 the book Tribes is about leading a group connected by an idea and a leader (S016, S038). By 2018 he says most of the time you find people already connected and lead or merely serve them (S002). In 2026 (newer) he says he would add, in capital letters, that you do not have a tribe; at best you narrate for one that would exist without you, and he regrets the misuse of the word (S019, paraphrase).
- **"Just ship it" became "merely ship it".** In 2017 his line is plain: "But now it's so easy to get to market that shipping is what matters." (S026) In 2020 he asks people to replace "just" with "merely" (S004), and in 2026 (newer) he denies ever saying the first version: "So I have never said just ship it. I say merely ship it." (S015) The change narrows "ship" to work that meets spec, offered without attachment.
- **Free.** In 2011 he warns that free widens the gap, because many people will never pay once a free version exists: "we've actually amplified the gap between free and paid." (S077) In 2013 and 2014 he still backs free for the unknown: "If you're an up-and-coming band building an audience, then yes, free, free, free." (S076) and "If you're unknown and making a digital good, it makes a lot of sense to charge zero, because it's free marketing" (S074). In 2026 (newer) he warns against making free content for platforms as a business plan (S015). His stable line: free to be discovered, then wrap the idea in something that cannot be free (S077).
- **Advertising and marketing spend.** He mostly treats paid interruption as the dying model (S016, S019, S029) and calls the scarcity era of attention over as of 2025 (S019). But in 2019 he warns: "often, it's the underspenders that are in real trouble" (S069), and in 2026 he says a product with neither economic nor social tension needs advertising, and so a budget (S056, paraphrase). Read him as anti-interruption, not anti-spend.
- **Copy or be original.** He tells small businesses to copy a proven model (S008, S017, S053), yet built his name on remarkable work and says total originality loses while rhyming with what came before wins (S027). Inferred from S008, S027, S053: copy the structure, make the thing people talk about remarkable.
- **Advice built from his own path.** Much of the guidance (daily blog, books with an audience that already trusts him, saying no to stages) comes from a writer and speaker with decades of earned trust (S027, S041). His sizes for "enough" are small (S004, S011, S028), and he says outright that staying at the start of the adoption curve is fine (S023). Inferred from S023, S027, S035: his lens fits bootstrapped, trust-led businesses better than venture-scale plans that need the mass market.
