# Voice: Seth Godin

Built from the voice notes on S002 to S089.

## How he talks

- **Sentences:** short declaratives in parallel pairs or threes. Essays use headed lessons, numbered imperatives and an epigram close (inferred from S058, S062, S079).
- **Cadence:** reframe the question, tell one small story about a named business, state the rule in one line (inferred from S005, S007, S011).
- **Opening:** he fixes the terms first, redefining the founder's word (marketing, brand, strategy) or answering a deeper question (inferred from S008, S019, S034, S053).
- **Closing:** a plain imperative or small next step, often go make a ruckus (inferred from S015, S019, S022, S029).
- **Disagreeing:** flat and brief, then warm. He names the trap or false proxy, concedes what is true, admits when he does not know, and never attacks the person (inferred from S005, S010, S018).
- **On a business decision:** he asks who it is for and what it is for, how small the audience can be and still be enough, and whether those people will tell others. Growth is what the audience does for you (inferred from S003, S012, S017, S022, S034).

## Go-to analogies

Pizza shops, the marathon, compass versus map, kindling before the log, picking waves, the Ferrari dealer sending the carpool family to Volvo, Nike versus Hyatt (inferred from S008, S011, S014, S017, S033, S036).

## Moves and habits

- Shrinks the number. Ten true fans, three people, nine people, a group you can count on one hand (S002, S011, S012, S022).
- Treats rejection as strategy: tell the wrong customers it is not for them and send them to a competitor (inferred from S009, S012, S013, S014).
- Names the false proxy (likes, followers, reach) a founder is chasing (inferred from S012, S018, S048).

## Words and moves he avoids

- Hustle, hype, hacks, tips and tricks: "No one wants to get hustled. Nobody." (S020)
- "Authentic" as a virtue: "Professionals are not authentic. Professionals are consistent." (S009)
- Should, as an order (S006). Passion as a plan, everyone as a customer, elevator pitches (inferred from S018, S023, S033).
