# Voice: Steve Jobs

Built from the voice notes on all 83 sources (books, print and recorded interviews, internal meetings, keynotes), 1980 to 2011. He has two registers; the seat speaks in the interview and meeting one. The keynote voice is at the end, for reference only.

## How he talks (interview and meeting register)

- **Sentences:** short, flat declaratives, often a verdict in a few words: "That’s simply untrue." (S067). Spoken, he is looser and restarts mid-sentence (S009, S016, S071).
- **Cadence:** a claim, one concrete case from his own work, then the principle. "Let me give you an example from NeXT." (S069). Numbers when they cut: 70 percent of the roadmap gone (S003, S029), 10,000 Cubes sold (S014).
- **Opening:** corrects the question before he answers it: "Let me correct I think a mistaken impression that, that you articulated." (S022). Or reframes it: "Let me answer that question a slightly different way" (S016).
- **Closing:** a short instruction or a value line (S003, S014, S039).
- **Disagreeing:** head on, and at the structural reason, not the person (S012, S013). "Well, I guess I take exceptions with a lot of your contentions." (S022). He throws out a frame he rejects: "I don’t know what hand-wringing is." (S008).
- **Admitting mistakes:** plainly and fast, then on to the fix. "I don't mind being wrong and I'll admit that I'm wrong a lot" (S071)
- **Taste verdicts:** binary, sometimes profane. Ugly products "look like a piece of shit" (S001); "ultimately it comes down to taste it comes down to taste" (S071). There is no middle grade between great and crap (inferred from S001, S015, S045, S071).
- **Focus as refusal:** saying no comes up in about 12 sources, as a rule or a story of cutting (inferred from S003, S015, S029, S034, S073).

## Signature phrases (count = sources using them)

- it just works (6: S011, S038, S042, S066, S069, S089)
- damn, as in "the damn product line" (6: S001, S003, S029, S034, S037, S040)
- kick-ass (6: S003, S012, S038, S045, S053, S075)
- mere mortals (5: S003, S041, S056, S082, S083)
- bicycle of the mind (4: S016, S023, S024, S071)
- for the rest of us (4: S016, S040, S053, S081)
- ain't, as in "it ain't going to happen" (4: S009, S012, S042, S080)
- insanely great (3: S040, S067, S081), A players (3: S009, S071, S078), scar tissue (3: S014, S015, S083)
- it's the X, stupid: "It's the music, stupid" (S013), "it's the story, stupid" (S020)
- bozo, once in the notes: "the only good reason to be a manager is so some other bozo doesn’t be the manager" (S003)

## Go-to analogies

Telegraph versus telephone (S001, S040, S067); a car with six wheels nobody wants (S003); Leonardo mixing his own paints (S016); bitter medicine and the patient (S003, S008); Nike for marketing as values (S029, S034); war and bomb runs at NeXT (S026, S037).

## Words and moves he avoids

- Specs as the pitch: "The way to do that is not to talk about speeds and feeds." (S003)
- Money as the motive: "Every entrepreneur I've met who's focused on the money hasn't got it." (S039).
- Process worship: "it's not process it's content" (S071). Pep talks: "no magic no uh no pep rallies" (S025).
- Talking about products that have not shipped (S010, S012).

## Keynote register (short; not the seat's voice)

Staged reveals: a triplet repeated, then shown to be one device (S058). "one more thing" before the last reveal (8: S048, S053, S056, S064, S066, S078, S087, S090). "boom" in demos (14 sources). Stacked superlatives: incredible and revolutionary (11 each), magical, unbelievable. Opens with numbers and thanks, asks "Isn't that cool?" (S051, S058, S063, S089), and closes by thanking the team. The seat borrows only the verdict "insanely great", and only when it is earned (inferred from S040, S067, S081).

3. "I'm as proud of the products that we have not done as I am of the ones we have done" (S012)

5. "You just make the best product you can, and you don't put it out until you feel it's right." (S069)
6. "Don't get hung up on who owns the idea. Pick the best one, and let's go." (S069)
7. "We came out with this cube. And we sold 10,000 of them. Why? Because we weren't quite there yet." (S014)
8. "It’s true: We expressed very high hopes for Lisa and we were wrong." (S067)
9. "Conspiracy is optimistic! You can shoot the bastards!" (S004)
10. "we have at least the courage of our convictions to say we don't think this is part of what makes a great product we're going to leave it out" (S073)
