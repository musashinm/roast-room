# Principles: Jason Lemkin

28 decision rules. "Sources" is the number of distinct sources that state or apply the rule (ids listed). Confidence: high = 3 or more sources, medium = 2, low = 1. Built from 95 sources, 2012 to 2026. Rules 1 to 18 are the SaaS sales playbook, mostly 2012 to 2024. Rules 19 to 28 are the AI-agent era, 2025 to 2026; where a newer rule changes an older one, it says so.

1. **The founder sells first: close the first ten customers yourself.** "95 times out of 100, you've got to find a way at least to close the first 10 customers yourself." (S046) "the biggest mistake folks make when it's early is they hire sales people to get them sales that that's the biggest mistake" (S059) Sources: 13 (S020, S036, S046, S059, S063, S072, S077, S081, S082, S083, S086, S090, S093). Confidence: high.

2. **Hire two reps, not one, before any VP of Sales.** "whatever you do don't hire one hire two" (S081) "If you hire a VP of sales before then, it's approaching 100% chance of failure." (S046) Sources: 10 (S004, S024, S046, S059, S066, S071, S080, S081, S082, S092). Confidence: high.

3. **Hire a salesperson you would buy from.** This is his rule for the first five or six reps. "as a founder do not hire a rep you wouldn't buy from" (S059). "hire two salespeople you'd buy from and you will become an expert too" (S088) Sources: 7 (S046, S059, S066, S067, S077, S088, S090). Confidence: high.

4. **Match the seller's past ACV to yours, not the industry.** "First, the ACV controls everything else." (S050) "don't overvalue domain expertise right sales is sales" (S081) Sources: 8 (S025, S046, S049, S050, S059, S066, S081, S090). Confidence: high.

5. **Never hire a sales leader who has not learned and demoed your product.** "never give anyone an offer letter before they give you a demo" (S077) "Never ever ever never ever hire VP of sales who doesn't really understand your product during the interview process." (S067) Sources: 7 (S046, S050, S063, S067, S077, S090, S047). Confidence: high.

6. **The VP of Sales must be willing to sell and visit customers first.** "do not hire a VP a sales that won't carry a bag that won't sell sell themselves" (S077) "meet fraking customers the first two weeks do not make the hire" (S090) Sources: 6 (S046, S067, S077, S090, S093, S020). Confidence: high.

7. **A VP has to show lift in one cycle, or it never comes.** "if a VP of sales doesn't make a difference in 30 days, they never will." (S049) "if that person can't come in and increase the revenue in one sales cycle that comes out of them it is hopeless" (S081) Sources: 10 (S020, S030, S031, S049, S050, S069, S081, S082, S083, S089). Confidence: high.

8. **A mis-hire is the hirer's fault and costs about a year.** "if you hire someone and they fail, it's all your fault." (S046) "you lose a year with every bad VP you hire" (S054) Sources: 5 (S046, S054, S063, S067, S090). Confidence: high.

9. **Do not hire for the logo.** "Don't fall in love with the fact that they worked at Slack" (S050) "would you have still hired them if they didn't work there" (S077) Sources: 7 (S020, S049, S050, S067, S077, S083, S047). Confidence: high.

10. **Do not settle to end the search; hire the great one when you meet her.** "The biggest mistake we all make is we get tired. We settle." (S067) "you should hire her instantly even if you're not ready hire her tomorrow" (S088) Sources: 5 (S067, S088, S090, S092, S080). Confidence: high.

11. **Hire a marketing leader who owns a number, early.** "find someone that's held a number at their prior job" (S059) "hire her early don't just wait for this VP of Sales" (S080) Sources: 9 (S020, S049, S050, S059, S077, S078, S080, S083, S092). Confidence: high.

12. **After the first VP arrives, the founder stays in sales.** "you never get the time back." (S020) "to spend just as much time in sales after you hire someone as you did before it's just your time should change" (S090) Sources: 6 (S020, S063, S077, S083, S086, S090). Confidence: high.

13. **Pay sellers well and keep the plan simple; do not cap the upside.** "Your best AE should drive an M6 convertible." (S050) "And please don’t cap the upside ." (S024) Sources: 7 (S024, S027, S029, S046, S049, S050, S079). Confidence: high.

14. **Over-invest in customer success; retention is the compounding engine.** "when in doubt over invest in customer success" (S083) "over invest in customer success make your friggin customers happy" (S091) Sources: 8 (S015, S032, S033, S059, S080, S083, S086, S091). Confidence: high.

15. **Take friction out of buying, from first touch to renewal.** "The best way to close a customer is to remove every bit of friction in the process, right?" (S063) "time kills deals" (S080) Sources: 5 (S063, S080, S087, S089, S057). Confidence: high.

16. **Raise price rather than hold a cheap SMB model; do not raise it for nothing.** "You just gotta raise prices . Even if the competition is cheaper. Even if your customers say they can’t afford it." (S009) "everyone raised prices without earning it" (S077) Sources: 6 (S008, S009, S062, S077, S079, S086). Confidence: high. Both views are his: raise price (2015), but a 2025 to 2026 caution that stacked increases without value get a vendor dropped.

17. **Do not cut your way to growth; marketing is the future.** "when you cut salespeople you cut your present when you cut marketing you cut your future" (S077) "you can't just cut your way to growth" (S091) Sources: 3 (S063, S077, S091). Confidence: high.

18. **Churn and retention decide whether it is software.** "if the churn is anything more than 3 or 4% a month it's not even software anymore" (S052) "Collect more than 100% of your MRR." (S020) Sources: 6 (S015, S020, S052, S058, S059, S089). Confidence: high.

19. **If you cannot sell it yourself, an AI cannot sell it for you.** "if you can't sell it yourself, the AI can't sell it for you." (S068) "If your outbound didn’t work with humans, AI won’t save it." (S037) Sources: 6 (S037, S064, S068, S072, S047, S055). Confidence: high.

20. **Train the agent yourself, daily, for about 30 days. Never buy and walk away.** "Every day you got to work with the agent until it works reliably." (S055) "if you just buy one of these products and disappear, you will have zero ROI." (S047) Sources: 9 (S047, S055, S057, S065, S068, S072, S073, S076, S095). Confidence: high.

21. **Pick the vendor by who will deploy it, and try two, not ten.** "don't sign the contract until you talk to your forward deployed engineer or sales agent." (S055) "just do the two and just do it and just try them both" (S076) Sources: 5 (S037, S047, S055, S072, S076). Confidence: high.

22. **Buy agents; do not build GTM software yourself.** "If you can buy a great agentic product, buy it." (S057) "None of the GTM stuff we built ourselves. Don't build it yourself. You're not Versel." (S047) Sources: 4 (S047, S057, S064, S065). Confidence: high.

23. **AI features alone do not count; know which budget you replace.** "AI features don't count." (S075) "now every founder needs to actually for real know where budget's coming from you've got to know" (S053) Sources: 7 (S026, S042, S053, S056, S068, S072, S075). Confidence: high.

24. **Automate inbound first.** "you should truly automate inbound" (S084) "Every company has inbound, so this is where we’d tell most teams to start." (S045) Sources: 5 (S045, S055, S076, S084, S095). Confidence: high.

25. **Be honest with the team that AI changes jobs, and fire the people who will not try.** "I don't think dancing around it is the right answer in your organization." (S047) "if folks don't want to adapt, they won't have a future." (S072) Sources: 4 (S047, S072, S073, S074). Confidence: high. This is a 2025 to 2026 stance and the harshest in the corpus; it comes from running SaaStr's own agent team, not a survey.

26. **Do not convert the installed base to a new pricing model overnight.** "Do not convert the base, in general." (S044) "They jack up prices without delivering more value. They cut CS headcount to hit EBITDA targets." (S042) Sources: 2 (S042, S044). Confidence: medium.

27. **Compare yourself to competitors, not only to last year.** "did we gain or lose market share this year" (S075) "if you don't compare yourself to competitors you're not being honest" (S051) Sources: 2 (S051, S075). Confidence: medium.

28. **Sell when the offer is clearly good in a hot market.** Older (2012, S001): sell only when the price is clearly above what the company is worth, or you will want it back: "But understand it really goes best when you sell that ’84 Fiero on the lot for $19,995." (S001) Older (2020, S061): with a great business, aim for the huge outcome. Newer (2024, S051): "if you get a good offer and it's in bullish times take it take it because it may be a decade" (S051). Sources: 3 (S001, S051, S061). Confidence: high. Thin by design; inferred from S001, S051, S061 that his lean moved toward taking good offers.
