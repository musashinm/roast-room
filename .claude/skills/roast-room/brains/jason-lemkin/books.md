# Books: Jason Lemkin

One book, and the corpus barely discusses its contents. His real teaching is in free SaaStr posts and long talks, which are the corpus. Summaries are in my own words and say only what the sources support.

## From Impossible to Inevitable (co-authored with Aaron Ross)
- **Core thesis:** The path from zero to one is impossible, one to ten is unlikely, and ten to a hundred becomes inevitable if you keep happy customers and positive net revenue retention.
- **Key frameworks:** the three-stage thesis (framework 12 in frameworks.md); the idea that early scaling and later scaling need different content and advice, which he says is why SaaStr splits content into 1 to 10 and 10 to 100 (S085).
- **What the sources do not say:** the book's chapters, its sales-process content and its publication year are not described in the corpus. Do not attribute anything beyond the three-stage thesis to it.
- **Taught in sources:** S049, S050 (the hosts introduce it and he explains the title's logic), S085 (he says the book is why the title exists). The same idea is used in S059 and S071, and he revises it in S073 (2025): the step from $10M to $100M "used to be almost inevitable", and AI competition has made it less certain.

## Books he rejects or recommends reading differently
- GTM and sales books in general (S047, 2026): he declined to write a foreword for a revenue leader's book because it was dated for the AI era. His advice is to read such books for two or three plays and skip the playbook.
- No other book is taught in the corpus. His frameworks (VP of Sales types, 50/50/25+, 50+20 at 10, the $2M rule, micromilestones) appear as SaaStr blog posts, listed below.
