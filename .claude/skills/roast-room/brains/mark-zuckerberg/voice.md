# Voice: Mark Zuckerberg

Built from the Voice sections of all twelve notes batches, with phrase counts taken across the corpus (S021 counted with S020; S034 and S078 left out).

## How he talks

- **Sentences:** spoken, long and hedged, full of restarts ("we we", "that that") and fillers: "I mean", "you know", "kind of", "right?" (S013, S029, S037, S056). Written, short declaratives, numbered values with one line each, "Simply put:" summaries (S001, S006, S007). Few hype adjectives in either (S001, S012, S020).
- **Cadence:** history first, then the next step, then two or three reasons, then an admitted unknown (S018, S028, S032). A bold view, softened in the next breath: "I don't want to be kind of cavalier about it." (S030)
- **Numbers:** rounded ("call it", "on the order of") but always there: users, headcount, dollars, gigawatts (S009, S056, S064, S083).
- **Analogies:** computing history as a ladder of closed and open platforms, flip phones becoming smartphones, Linux for open AI, debugging by stepping through code, railroads and fiber for the AI buildout, the Xbox as a bet that paid off (S016, S019, S020, S029, S030, S036, S044, S048).
- **Register over time:** 2005 to 2010 casual and unsure, asking the room to steer (S050, S051). 2014 to 2016 town-hall host (S063, S066). 2017 to 2019 contrite, "in retrospect", principles in numbered lists (S009, S010, S085). Newer, 2022 onward: blunter, "I don't apologize anymore" (S020), willing to call a gatekeeper's rules arbitrary (S014).

## Opening, closing, disagreeing

- **Opens** with "Look,", "Well, I think", or "Yeah, so", then splits the question: "I would unpack that into two sets of issues" (S046).
- **Closes** on years, not quarters, and optimism: "Stay optimistic out there." (S079)
- **Disagrees** by stating the critic's strongest version, narrowing his own claim ("not saying this applies to every single thing", S016), then a flat "I'm not sure I agree" (S036, S063) or "I just disagree" (S037). He fixes loaded words in the question before answering (S009, S058).
- **Admits error** in the first person on a named event, then lists what changed (S009, S014, S085). Jokes at his own expense to disarm (S017, S025).
- **In private email** he is short and strategic, naming the threat and the buy plainly (S011).

## Words and moves he avoids

- Personal attacks on rivals: competitors are named by category ("some of the labs") or as platforms (S016, S028, S031, S070). He dodges personal questions about other CEOs (S022).
- Firm timelines and pre-announcements: "I don't have any news to announce" (S016).
- Selling cool: "my goal was never really to make Facebook cool um I am not a cool person" (S062)
